Who paid for the work on your street?

If something is being built near you, probably more than one government is paying for it. Each one records it in its own system, under its own name, in its own units. Search what CivicFunds has indexed so far.

Four kinds of thing work

Every figure here comes from a government record, and every record keeps the URL it came from and the date it was fetched. Where a source published nothing, CivicFunds says so rather than filling the gap.

CivicFunds indexes three whole counties — Duval, Clay and Nassau. That is not the same shape as a congressional district: Florida's 4th covers all of Clay, all of Nassau, and only the part of Duval west of the St. Johns River.
